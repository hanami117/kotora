Kotora for Windows.

Please report what goes wrong at https://github.com/hanami117/kotora/issues

Good to know:

- Kotora is installed for you only. Setup never asks for administrator rights.

- The programs are not signed yet. Windows may have said "Windows protected your PC" when
  you started this Setup; it may say so again for a new version.

- Closing Kotora's window keeps it running in the notification area (next to the clock,
  maybe behind the ^ arrow), so it can see what you watch. Right-click its icon to quit.

- Your settings, your sign-in and the saved copy of your list are kept in your user profile
  (%APPDATA%\kotora and %LOCALAPPDATA%\kotora). Uninstalling Kotora keeps them unless you
  say otherwise.

- Kotora checks once a day whether a new version is out and tells you. It downloads
  nothing by itself. You can switch the check off in Settings, General.
